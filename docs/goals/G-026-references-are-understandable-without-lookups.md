# Understand references while reading

People reading Cliewen documents, release notes, CLI messages or an agent's handoff need to understand the relevant purpose and action from that text. The owner requested the same method across these surfaces: explain meaning inline, link with descriptive words for optional detail, and keep identities secondary where they help exact identification.

Success means a reader can explain why a reference matters without opening it. Machine-facing identities remain stable and canonical. A table of IDs or copied source documents does not replace the needed explanation.
