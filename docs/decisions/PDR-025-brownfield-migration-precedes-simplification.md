# PDR-025 — Truthful brownfield migration precedes simplification

## Context and problem statement

The brownfield assessment found that extraction could lose identity reservations, evidence state, active-work traceability, and operational carriers; simplification could not defer a migration contract that would let an extraction look green while changing what the source proves.

## Decision outcome

**P-011 is the migration-only campaign, and simplification follows it.** [PDR-026](PDR-026-campaigns-close-on-re-derived-evidence.md) supersedes only the clause that named P-012 as the simplification campaign: P-012 closes the migration gap on re-derived evidence and P-013 is the simplification campaign. P-011 remains `draft` until P-010 completes and proceeds from identity preservation through evidence and carrier parity to disposable end-to-end fixtures.

Brownfield identity is preserved rather than inferred from visible sequences. P-011 defines the permanent machine-checked ledger and opaque-token grammar; any semantic change to identity or evidence remains a full change with its own decision and human acceptance under C-013.

## Rejected: retain P-011 for simplification

That would leave a known unsafe extraction rule behind an unrelated campaign and delay the higher-risk migration boundary.

## Carrier

P-010's forward-plan boundary, P-011's scope and milestones, the plans index, and this record carry the sequencing; P-011's contract decisions update affected carriers under PDR-019.
