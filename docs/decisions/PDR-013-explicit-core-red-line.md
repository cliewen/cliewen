# PDR-013 — Cliewen has an explicit core behind a red line

> **Acceptance scope amended by [PDR-042](PDR-042-routing-recommends-contract-aware-effort.md):** core changes are recommended for the full loop, but an explicit user choice may take the simple route with its risk recorded.

> **The thread endpoint is refined by [PDR-019](PDR-019-methodology-contract-carriers-move-together.md):** the protected thread ends in acceptance evidence, including classified executable evidence and genuine Human proof in the acceptance brief.

> **The carrier test is qualified by [PDR-029](PDR-029-simplification-tests-by-surface.md):** the core test governs rule-bearing artifacts and meanings; carrier prose also needs a live corpus trace, a single reading-path rule, and an admitted check.

## Context and problem statement

Without a load-bearing definition, simplification treats a guide typo and a change to merge semantics alike, while adopters cannot distinguish what they may extend from what a green judge or accepted merge must continue to mean.

## Decision outcome

**Cliewen's core is the verifiable thread, the human acceptance boundary, and `clue validate` as deterministic judge; everything else is periphery.** The thread runs from goal to plan to change to capability to acceptance criterion to acceptance evidence. A change that alters the meaning of any core element requires an explicit decision record and human acceptance under the red line; periphery never constrains the core.

Adopters may add their own artifacts and types under `/docs`; the core defines what the thread, human boundary, and judge mean rather than a fixed blessed-type list. The red-line rule is carried by AGENTS.md, C-013, and ARCH-003, with methodology carriers updated together under PDR-019.
