# PDR-050 — Analysis evidence states its environment and population boundary

## Context

A prepared build can be mistaken for clean onboarding evidence, an informal population can bias a numerical claim, and adoption scaffolding can hide governance changes.

## Decision

Analysis classifies verification as clean disposable or prepared and names every prerequisite of a prepared environment. Statistical evidence names its versioned corpus, population, eligibility, exclusions, method, uncertainty, and deterministic-versus-quality boundary. Adoption analysis names the governance or process changes it introduces rather than describing scaffolding as neutral. Findings distinguish observed, inferred, and unverified claims and do not infer maintainer intent from repository activity.
