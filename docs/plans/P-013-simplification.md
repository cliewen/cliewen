# P-013 — Cliewen is simplified against a stated criterion

This campaign is [P-012](P-012-migration-gap-closes-on-evidence.md)'s successor and is open. [PDR-026](../decisions/PDR-026-campaigns-close-on-re-derived-evidence.md) moved simplification here from P-012; [PDR-025](../decisions/PDR-025-brownfield-migration-precedes-simplification.md)'s reason for deferring it in the first place still holds, and this file exists so that the deferral is a named destination rather than a phrase in successive campaigns' out-of-scope sections.

Simplification has been deferred by every campaign that named it, each one to its own successor, three times over. That is worth stating plainly, because a thing deferred three times is either not actually wanted or has never been given a shape it could be executed in. This campaign's first problem is the second one.

## The material this campaign has to work with

[PDR-013](../decisions/PDR-013-explicit-core-red-line.md) already supplies the criterion — *does the core need it?* — and records why simplification kept stalling without one: every debate was argued case by case, protection had no boundary, and uniform protection turned out to be uniform friction. What the criterion has never been is systematically applied to a measured surface.

That criterion is not sufficient on its own, and [PDR-029](../decisions/PDR-029-simplification-tests-by-surface.md) records why. Applied to Cliewen's own methodology carriers it passes almost everything, because nearly every rule a skill states genuinely is needed by the core. So this campaign carries two tests, chosen by surface: *does the core need it?* for rules, artifact types, required fields, commands, and checks, and, for carrier prose, whether every statement traces to a live corpus artifact, states its rule once in the reading path, and admits checking. PDR-029 fixes the campaign's measure of success as the set of rules that survive those conditions — traceable, unduplicated, checkable, ordered by how binding they are, and consistent with one another — and explicitly not word count, because the second test sometimes makes a passage longer and a campaign scored on size would repair duplication by worsening compression.

**The shipped skills are this campaign's primary object.** They are what a reviewer evaluating Cliewen reads most closely, and they are read adversarially: the question asked of a skill is not whether it is short but, of a particular sentence, *why do you have this statement?* The only answer that survives is a corpus artifact the reader can open. Cliewen already demands that discipline of a product — every behavior traceable to a criterion, every criterion to a capability, every capability to a goal — and its own instructions are the one surface where it was never applied. So the first two milestones are about the six skills and the routing hub alone: every statement traced, then the set trimmed and reordered against that register. M-064 carries the remaining carrier prose — the public guide, contributor guidance, and CLI text — so no carrier leaves this campaign unscored.

Order is part of the object, not a presentation detail. A carrier that states its inviolable rules after the procedure they constrain has arranged itself so the cheapest reading is the least safe one, and an agent that acts before finishing the file will have read the steps and not the prohibition. This repository's own skills currently do exactly that.

**The campaign asks rather than assumes.** A statement that traces to nothing may be waste, or it may be a real rule nobody ever wrote down; a statement that traces cleanly may still rest on a decision that has outlived its reason. Neither is settled by an agent. Both become open questions for the human — is this rule still needed, does it still make sense, is it important enough to bind every change, and why — and the answers become decisions. The campaign's target is a smaller set of rules that hold together, not a larger set that overlaps: two rules covering one situation is how ambiguity enters a methodology, and an ambiguous rule is obeyed inconsistently while appearing to be obeyed.

The two surfaces differ in how safely they can be cut, and that difference governs the whole campaign. Carrier prose is instruction: it can be rewritten, merged, or moved as long as every rule survives in the reading path. The corpus is the memory multiple humans and coding agents share, and its obligation is that a different reader, later, recovers the same meaning — so reducing what the corpus can remember is not simplification, whatever the read cost. M-064 is the one milestone that reaches that surface directly, and the part of it that does is scoped to make removal *legible* rather than to remove.

The measured cost is in [AN-006](../analysis/AN-006-plain-change-overhead.md) (overhead on changes that carry no meaning), [AN-010](../analysis/AN-010-adopter-change-overhead.md) (what a change costs an adopter rather than an authoring repository), and [AN-012](../analysis/AN-012-adopter-configuration-cost.md) (what adoption costs before any change happens). [AN-008](../analysis/AN-008-methodology-critiques.md)'s pattern C names the structural half: the graph only accumulates, and a corpus with no compaction path grows monotonically whatever its rules say.

[AN-013](../analysis/AN-013-distributed-work-and-evidence-boundaries.md)'s findings are a different matter and are recorded here so they are not lost. P-010's M-050 evidence names F1, F2's first half, and F3 as open findings feeding P-011, and P-011 closed without addressing any of them — but their histories differ, and this campaign owes each one a determination rather than a repetition of that sentence. P-009 declined F1's dependent-change candidate outright rather than deferring it, so what is open there is the decline's stated cost, not an unbuilt interface. [ADR-040](../decisions/ADR-040-qualified-external-references.md) consumed two of AN-013's candidates at once under P-009's M-044, so both remaining findings are narrower than they first read: it answered F3's qualified-reference candidate, leaving as open only whatever that decision did not reach, and it supplied the named-but-locally-unproven form F2 asked for, which leaves as F2's residue what AN-013 actually scopes to its first half — one repository's emitted CI wall kept in step by hand. That residue has never had a route at all. This campaign establishes for each whether it is answered, declined with a recorded cost, or genuinely open, and adopts or re-routes what remains; each of them adds machinery rather than removing it, which is exactly why the determination belongs here rather than in a milestone that assumes the answer. None of the three is dropped silently.

## Milestones

Milestone numbering continues corpus-global numbering from [P-012](P-012-migration-gap-closes-on-evidence.md)'s M-061. The order is load-bearing twice over. The skills come first because they are the campaign's primary object, not because they are the easiest surface. And within that, M-062 registers before M-063 trims, because every previous attempt at simplification argued case by case and stalled — a statement cannot be defended or removed on merit until someone has written down what it is for.

| ID | Milestone (exit criterion) | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| M-062 | **Every statement in the shipped skills and the routing hub is traced, and the unanswerable ones reach a human**: a spike walks the six generated skills and `AGENTS.md` statement by statement and records, for each, the live corpus artifact it traces to — a decision, constraint, goal, or criterion — or the fact that it traces to nothing; whether a live carrier already states that rule in the same reading path; and whether a reader can determine its satisfaction. The register also assesses order, reporting every statement that binds absolutely but is read after the procedure it constrains. Duplication is counted per reading path rather than per file, so [ADR-021](../decisions/ADR-021-generated-standalone-skills.md)'s deliberate file-level repetition is not scored as a defect. Two classes are escalated rather than decided: a statement that traces to nothing, and a statement whose decision may itself have outlived its reason. Each becomes an open question naming what it traces to or fails to, what removing it would cost, and whether the underlying rule is still important enough to bind every change — answered by a human, recorded under [C-011](../constraints/C-011-decision-records-typed.md). The register is written to be read by someone who did not build it. The milestone also settles what durable form the register should take. **The carriers themselves stay clean: no trace, citation, or marker is added to a skill or to `AGENTS.md`.** The register is a separate artifact in this repository, and the design problem that creates is drift — a map kept beside the prose it describes, with no mechanism, is the second stored representation [PDR-028](../decisions/PDR-028-derived-report-is-not-a-committed-registry.md) refused, and it goes stale on the first skill edit with nothing reporting it. So the milestone fixes what counts as a single statement precisely enough that a tool can segment carrier prose the same way twice, reports the population that definition yields, and recommends how a register row stays bound to a statement it does not annotate — including whether an edited statement should invalidate its row and force the rule to be restated. Building it is a plan revision adding a milestone, backed by its own decision record and human acceptance — this milestone recommends and does not build. The register also records overlap candidates: pairs of statements carrying distinct obligations over one situation with no stated precedence, which M-063 merges and which no other column captures. ## Mutation rules

Once this campaign opens and its milestone table exists, status and evidence fields in that table may mutate in an implementing change's merge digest. Everything else changes only through a declared plan revision backed by a decision record routed by reversal cost. Plan adjustments are decisions.
