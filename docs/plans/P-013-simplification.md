# P-013 — Cliewen is simplified against a stated criterion

This campaign is [P-012](P-012-migration-gap-closes-on-evidence.md)'s successor and is open. [PDR-026](../decisions/PDR-026-campaigns-close-on-re-derived-evidence.md) moved simplification here from P-012; [PDR-025](../decisions/PDR-025-brownfield-migration-precedes-simplification.md)'s reason for deferring it in the first place still holds, and this file exists so that the deferral is a named destination rather than a phrase in successive campaigns' out-of-scope sections.

Simplification has been deferred by every campaign that named it, each one to its own successor, three times over. That is worth stating plainly, because a thing deferred three times is either not actually wanted or has never been given a shape it could be executed in. This campaign's first problem is the second one.

## The material this campaign has to work with

[PDR-013](../decisions/PDR-013-explicit-core-red-line.md) already supplies the criterion — *does the core need it?* — and records why simplification kept stalling without one: every debate was argued case by case, protection had no boundary, and uniform protection turned out to be uniform friction. What the criterion has never been is systematically applied to a measured surface.

That criterion is not sufficient on its own, and [PDR-029](../decisions/PDR-029-simplification-tests-by-surface.md) records why. Applied to Cliewen's own methodology carriers it passes almost everything, because nearly every rule a skill states genuinely is needed by the core. So this campaign carries two tests, chosen by surface: *does the core need it?* for rules, artifact types, required fields, commands, and checks, and, for carrier prose, whether every statement earns its place, states its rule once in the reading path, and admits checking. PDR-029 fixes the campaign's measure of success as the set of statements that survive those three conditions, and explicitly not word count, because the second test sometimes makes a passage longer and a campaign scored on size would repair duplication by worsening compression.

**The shipped skills are this campaign's primary object.** They are what a reviewer evaluating Cliewen reads most closely, and they are read adversarially: the question asked of a skill is not whether it is short but, of a particular sentence, *why do you have this statement?* A methodology whose own instructions cannot answer that has lost the argument before any of its rules are examined. So the first two milestones are about the six skills and the routing hub alone — every statement registered and justified, then the set trimmed against that register — and the rest of the surface follows. Each surviving statement must make sense to a human reviewer and to a coding agent on its own terms, evident from what Cliewen is for rather than from the history of how the sentence came to be written.

The two surfaces differ in how safely they can be cut, and that difference governs the whole campaign. Carrier prose is instruction: it can be rewritten, merged, or moved as long as every rule survives in the reading path. The corpus is the memory multiple humans and coding agents share, and its obligation is that a different reader, later, recovers the same meaning — so reducing what the corpus can remember is not simplification, whatever the read cost. M-064 is the one milestone that reaches that surface directly, and the part of it that does is scoped to make removal *legible* rather than to remove.

The measured cost is in [AN-006](../analysis/AN-006-plain-change-overhead.md) (overhead on changes that carry no meaning), [AN-010](../analysis/AN-010-adopter-change-overhead.md) (what a change costs an adopter rather than an authoring repository), and [AN-012](../analysis/AN-012-adopter-configuration-cost.md) (what adoption costs before any change happens). [AN-008](../analysis/AN-008-methodology-critiques.md)'s pattern C names the structural half: the graph only accumulates, and a corpus with no compaction path grows monotonically whatever its rules say.

[AN-013](../analysis/AN-013-distributed-work-and-evidence-boundaries.md)'s findings are a different matter and are recorded here so they are not lost. P-010's M-050 evidence names F1, F2's first half, and F3 as open findings feeding P-011, and P-011 closed without addressing any of them — but their histories differ, and this campaign owes each one a determination rather than a repetition of that sentence. P-009 declined F1's dependent-change candidate outright rather than deferring it, so what is open there is the decline's stated cost, not an unbuilt interface. [ADR-040](../decisions/ADR-040-qualified-external-references.md) consumed two of AN-013's candidates at once under P-009's M-044, so both remaining findings are narrower than they first read: it answered F3's qualified-reference candidate, leaving as open only whatever that decision did not reach, and it supplied the named-but-locally-unproven form F2 asked for, which leaves as F2's residue what AN-013 actually scopes to its first half — one repository's emitted CI wall kept in step by hand. That residue has never had a route at all. This campaign establishes for each whether it is answered, declined with a recorded cost, or genuinely open, and adopts or re-routes what remains; each of them adds machinery rather than removing it, which is exactly why the determination belongs here rather than in a milestone that assumes the answer. None of the three is dropped silently.

## Milestones

Milestone numbering continues corpus-global numbering from [P-012](P-012-migration-gap-closes-on-evidence.md)'s M-061. The order is load-bearing twice over. The skills come first because they are the campaign's primary object, not because they are the easiest surface. And within that, M-062 registers before M-063 trims, because every previous attempt at simplification argued case by case and stalled — a statement cannot be defended or removed on merit until someone has written down what it is for.

| ID | Milestone (exit criterion) | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| M-062 | **Every statement in the shipped skills and the routing hub is registered and justified**: a spike walks the six generated skills and `AGENTS.md` statement by statement and records, for each, the rule or decision it serves, whether a live carrier already states that rule in the same reading path, and whether a reader can determine its satisfaction. A statement whose justification is absent, circular, only historical, or reducible to "it was already written" is a removal candidate. Duplication is counted per reading path rather than per file, so [ADR-021](../decisions/ADR-021-generated-standalone-skills.md)'s deliberate file-level repetition is not scored as a defect. The register is the artifact a human reviewer reads to ask *why do you have this statement?* and get an answer, so it is written to be read by someone who did not build it. Nothing is removed by this milestone. | `todo` | |
| M-063 | **The shipped skills and routing hub carry only statements that earn their place**: the set is trimmed against M-062's register so that every surviving statement passes all three of [PDR-029](../decisions/PDR-029-simplification-tests-by-surface.md)'s carrier conditions, and every rule that a removed statement carried is still stated somewhere the reading path reaches — meaning is preserved even where wording is not. Because the skills are methodology carriers, this is a full-tier change that updates every live carrier including the generated trees and the scaffolded adopter copies, and the generator source is edited rather than its output. Evidence re-runs the register and shows no unjustified, duplicated-in-path, or uncheckable statement remaining, and states the surviving statement set rather than a word count. | `todo` | |
| M-064 | **The rest of the surface is scored and settled**: every remaining rule, artifact type, required field, command, and check is scored against *does the core need it?*, and each candidate is either carried out — with a guard proving that what the core needs still resolves — or declined with its cost stated, in the manner [PDR-026](../decisions/PDR-026-campaigns-close-on-re-derived-evidence.md) requires of a declined request. Where a removal requires it, this milestone also makes the corpus able to shrink legibly, closing [AN-008](../analysis/AN-008-methodology-critiques.md)'s pattern C: a retired or superseded artifact provably leaves the mandatory read path as `clue context` and `clue validate` compute it, a decision carries a supersession edge so that reversing one answers what was downstream of it, and the born-`inferred` population is bounded rather than monotonic. Because that reaches the shared-memory surface, nothing removed from the read path may become unrecoverable: every retired artifact's meaning stays reachable by a route this milestone's evidence names. A candidate touching the verifiable thread, the human merge boundary, or the judge carries its own decision record under [C-013](../constraints/C-013-core-changes-need-decision.md). No candidate is silently dropped. | `todo` | |
| M-065 | **[AN-013](../analysis/AN-013-distributed-work-and-evidence-boundaries.md)'s three open findings each carry a determination**: F1, F2's first half, and F3 are each answered with a named mechanism, declined with a recorded cost, or routed to a successor campaign as an explicit milestone. Each adds machinery rather than removing it, so each determination states why the addition is warranted inside a simplification campaign or why it is not. None is dropped silently, and the repetition of P-010's and P-011's "still open" sentence does not satisfy this milestone. | `todo` | |
| M-066 | **The campaign closes on re-derived evidence**: the cost surfaces [AN-006](../analysis/AN-006-plain-change-overhead.md), [AN-010](../analysis/AN-010-adopter-change-overhead.md), and [AN-012](../analysis/AN-012-adopter-configuration-cost.md) measured are measured again at head, and the result states what changed, what did not, and what the campaign declined — re-derived from the corpus and the tool rather than read off this table, as PDR-026 requires. This milestone's digest closes the campaign and designates a successor when one is decided. | `todo` | |

## Mutation rules

Once this campaign opens and its milestone table exists, status and evidence fields in that table may mutate in an implementing change's merge digest. Everything else changes only through a declared plan revision backed by a decision record routed by reversal cost. Plan adjustments are decisions.
