# CH-085 — Prioritize the migration-readiness campaign

## What

Create P-009 as an active, sequential campaign that closes the demonstrated gaps between Cliewen's current adoption promise and a truthful large-repository migration. The plan consumes AN-012's non-forking-wall and mechanical-upgrade candidates and AN-013's contradicted JVM carrier finding, adds the newly demonstrated stable-ID and merge-mode boundaries, and turns the unsupported-JVM-profile concern into a named-test carrier rather than reviving proximity comments. This change is plan-less because its product is the plan.

## Why

The current judge can require classified positive and negative evidence, but its JVM file-level cross-product can credit unrelated methods and therefore produce a false green. Brownfield extraction also rejects stable source identifiers whose prefix has multiple segments or whose numeric portion has a letter suffix. Both contradict the migration promise directly: one overstates proof and the other requires identity churn.

Two adopter-wide operational costs are already routed to P-009 by accepted analyses: editing the copied CI wall severs its upgrade channel, and a release that adds corpus obligations has no mechanical migration path. The operations guide also leaves the merge strategy implicit even though squash-merging a branch that creates and later deletes its proposal does not retain that proposal in `main` history. These gaps need an order that repairs provenance truth and false-green correctness before widening supported syntax and then removes recurring operational forks.

## Decision boundary

This change decides the campaign scope, priority, and exit criteria only. Each milestone remains a separate full Cliewen change from accepted `main` and routes its own lasting ADR or PDR. The plan does not choose a JVM parser, reusable-workflow contract, migration-file format, or squash-merge accommodation in advance of that milestone's design and evidence.

Permanent dual-running remains out of scope because two live systems of record would make accepted meaning ambiguous; the existing report-only extraction rehearsal is the reversible checkpoint. Structured `// AC:` proximity comments remain rejected by ADR-005; frameworks without native tags receive a stable named-executable profile or remain explicitly unsupported. General stacked-change and cross-repository-evidence interfaces remain deferred because AN-013 did not price a safe design and neither is required to make a single migration truthful.
