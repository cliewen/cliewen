# CH-076 — Reading and change cost are proportionate to the task

M-029 of [P-007](../../docs/plans/P-007-core-hardening.md). Agents are currently told to read the corpus entry point before every light or full change, even when one artifact and its linked neighborhood contain the relevant durable context. The CLI can validate the whole graph but cannot expose that focused slice, so readers either search manually or load more of the corpus than the task requires. The methodology also routes every product behavior change through the full loop without evidence from the history of an adopting product repository that the cost is proportionate for the modal change.

This change will add `clue context <id>` as a deterministic, transitive link-slice view and route repository guidance to it after initial tier classification. It will consume the parked `clue locate` idea rather than add a second discovery command. Acceptance criteria will define the command's boundary and focused positive and negative evidence will precede implementation.

The change will also measure Cliewen overhead in the pinned history of a real adopting product repository and preserve the spike as an analysis record. A PDR will use that evidence to decide whether behavior changes under existing acceptance criteria may use the light tier or whether the full loop remains mandatory for the modal change. The resulting tier rule will have one canonical template source, with generated skills and the public and repository-local carriers aligned from it.
