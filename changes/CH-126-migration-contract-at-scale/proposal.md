# CH-126 — Prove the migration contract at assessment scale

## Why

P-012/M-061 remains the campaign's final open milestone: its existing evidence is deliberately fixture-sized and therefore does not establish that the composed brownfield migration contract holds at the scale described by the originating assessment.

## What changes

Create a disposable, assessment-scale migration fixture and exercise the complete supported path against it: pinned release, report-only rehearsal, identity reservation across relevant namespaces, parity, carrier reconciliation, authorized mutation, and target validation. Add deterministic failure coverage at that size, record the resulting bounded evidence without naming a production adopter, and update the capability and P-012 digest so the campaign closes and designates P-013.

## Acceptance

The repository has a focused acceptance criterion and executable evidence for the assessment-scale composed migration path and its required deterministic failure modes. The permanent corpus records the result and P-012 has no unfinished milestone.
